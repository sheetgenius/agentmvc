# Draft handoff: private published articles

This is a proposed **fresh-agent change task**, not a frozen input or a scored check. Finalize and validate its behavior and checks before any handoff agent sees it. Its purpose is to test whether a new agent can find and change a rule across HTTP, jobs, sockets, and persisted data without reading the whole backend.

Add article visibility as `public` or `private`, independent of draft/published status. Creation defaults to public. A migration makes every article written by the earlier app public without rewriting its content. Only the author may change visibility. An author can read their own private article directly; anonymous callers, followers, and other signed-in users receive the existing 404 envelope for its slug. Ordinary article lists and feeds include published public articles only, and counts agree with returned pages. The tag discovery endpoint does not disclose a tag used only by private articles. The owner's export includes their private content. An active editing link remains a capability to read and edit its article; a revoked or rotated key loses access. Draft behavior and unrelated public article behavior remain as before.

Acceptance checks should create a private published article with a unique title and tag, then probe every entrance above using author, follower, stranger, and anonymous clients. Preserve a public article in a database created by the old version, migrate it with the new version, and prove it remains visible. Exercise an active link before and after revocation, including a socket already subscribed. Vary page sizes and catalog size when checking SQL statement counts; inspect representative query plans or rows visited to detect a single expensive aggregate hidden inside a fixed statement count.

The handoff measurement should record the fresh agent's time and tokens, files opened and edited, incorrect or missing rule-owner guesses, compile/check failures, source delta, independent development and production gates, and repeated runtime measurements. Keep the original expert build immutable; the handoff starts from a copy and is labeled as a separate phase.
